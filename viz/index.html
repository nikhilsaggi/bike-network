              What “cost” means

              Cost is how the router picks a route from A to B. Every block has a cost in each direction, and the “safest” route is the one whose blocks add up to the least cost. A block's cost is its length, scaled up by two things:

              1. The traffic penalty: how stressful the cars make the street. Any traffic adds a base amount, and heavier traffic, a higher speed limit or a truck route add more (the knobs below).
              2. The bike lane, if any: how much of that penalty it leaves. No lane leaves all of it, sharrows and painted lanes some, a protected lane none.

              cost = length × (1 + traffic penalty × share the lane leaves)

              So a protected lane, or a street with no cars, costs exactly its length. If a block is so stressful that walking the bike would be easier, or you can't ride it (wrong way on a one-way, or a footpath), it's priced as a walk instead.
